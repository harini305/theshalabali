At The Shala Bali, our Panchakarma Retreats offer a deeply restorative Ayurvedic healing experience designed to help you cleanse, rebalance and reconnect with your natural state of wellbeing.
Rooted in the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda, Panchakarma is one of the most profound and transformative healing processes for restoring balance to the body, calming the mind and supporting long-term vitality. More than simply a detox, Panchakarma is a personalised therapeutic journey that works to gently remove accumulated toxins, strengthen digestion, reset the nervous system and support the body’s own healing intelligence.
Held in the peaceful surroundings of The Shala Bali, our Panchakarma Retreats combine authentic Ayurvedic care, personalised treatment plans, nourishing food and the guidance of our experienced Ayurvedic doctor to create a truly supportive and high-quality healing experience.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long should I stay for a Panchakarma Retreat ?
The ideal length of a PanchaKarma Retreat is unique for everyone, we understand that everyone may not have a lot of time to take out from their daily life, so how long one should stay for a Panchakarma is personal, but durations of 7 days minimum is recommended and optimum up to 21 days. Other considerations are: current state of health and the level of cleansing or support needed. Some guests may benefit from a shorter introductory Ayurvedic cleanse, while others may choose a longer retreat for a deeper therapeutic process. We recommend speaking with us so we can help guide you toward the most suitable retreat length for your needs.
Is Panchakarma suitable for everyone?
PanchaKarma is a traditional therapeutic process, and in essence a beautiful & simple approach to leading a healthy daily life, all guests are treated specifically to their personal constitution and present condition, for this reason PanchaKarma is suitable for most people, but if you have a serious disease or ailment, please consult with our Ayurvedic doctor first to assess your needs.
What treatments are included in a Panchakarma Retreat?
Every retreat is personalized, so the type of treatments varies from guest to guest depending on your needs, your programme will include 2 body treatments per day, dietary and herbal support, cleansing therapies, rejuvenation therapies, activities & more
Will I receive a consultation with the Ayurvedic doctor?
Yes. Your retreat includes guidance from our experienced Ayurvedic doctor, who will assess your needs and oversee your personalised programme.
Is Panchakarma just a detox?
No. While Panchakarma does support cleansing and detoxification, it is much more than a detox. It is a traditional Ayurvedic healing process designed to remove toxins, restore balance, strengthen digestion, calm the nervous system and support deeper healing and rejuvenation.
What kind of food is served during the retreat?
Meals are designed to support the Panchakarma process and your individual needs as much as possible. Food is nourishing, digestible and aligned with Ayurvedic principles to help support cleansing, balance and recovery.
Can I do a Panchakarma Retreats if I am feeling stressed or burnt out?
Yes, many people choose Panchakarma because they are feeling depleted, overwhelmed, fatigued or out of balance. Ayurveda recognizes the close connection between stress, digestion, immunity and overall wellbeing, and a properly guided Pancha Karma retreat can be a powerful way to reset and restore.
How do I know if a Panchakarma Retreat is right for me?
If you are feeling out of balance, run down, depleted, burdened by stress, struggling with digestion, or simply ready for a deeper reset, a Pancha Karma Retreat may be a valuable healing experience. We recommend reaching out to us so we can help you determine whether the retreat is a good fit for your current needs and goals.

Bring your vision to life
The Shala Bali is a home for people from around the world to learn, share and preserve the ancient practices of Yoga and to experience the benefits of integrated wellness.
The Shala Bali was created by Yoga teachers for Yoga teachers and their groups. The serene and secluded nature environment is ideal for complete immersion and well equipped for trainings and courses.
Our experienced team will work with you to create a memorable and transformational experience for your guests.

Myself and my group of yogis were grateful to have a very relaxing time lazing by the salt water pool, and having long lazy swims in the sunshine. The staff were incredibly generous and helpful too. Accommodation options
Villa rooms
Enjoy modern Balinese inspired beauty. Our six Villa Rooms each have a unique design and outlook, some with peaceful jungle views, others overlooking the endless green rice paddies. Located close to the Yoga Shala and Shala Cafe. All villa rooms can be set up as twin or king.
Wooden Bungalows
Relax in rustic elegance. Our three Darshana Bungalows built from 100% recycled Javanese wood are a perfect hideaway. Each have a private garden lined with lush greenery and wild flowers. Sitting a stones throw from our beautiful Ayurvedic spa and Indian Cafe. All Darshana Bungalows can be set up as king or twin
Suite rooms (limited availability)
Experience a little extra. Our three suites each with attached living area and kitchen are only available during limited dates. The luxurious master rooms of the Kasih Villa, Ganapati Villa and Rahasia Villa. Two of the three master suites can be set up as twin or king.

Villa Kitty
A nursery for mothers, a hospital for the unwell and a safe haven for all Bali cats who are in desperate need of love and care. Villa Kitty has worked tirelessly to provide a shelter for the many many cats who have been abandoned to live in scary and painful situations. They are the loving hand that takes in cats of all shapes sizes and medical difficulties. The Shala Bali is ever grateful for their compassionate and for making Bali a better place for our fury fellow beings.

Guest testimonials
We take great pleasure in seeing our guests smiling and feeling well cared for. Here is what some of our visitors shared about their experience at The Shala Bali.
"I´ve been so lucky to stayed at the Shala Bali for four months in total since last year. This is the perfect environment for a yoga retreat. Is a paradise full of beauty and peace, where the lovely and smiley staff made us to feel as home. The Shala, the pool and all the rooms are amazing. Each room has a lovely design, very comfortable and charm, perfect to rest and relax. The restaurant is great; food is delicious… vegan and organic. What else can you ask for as a yogui? Everything at the same place: comfort, beauty, lovely people, swimming pool, amazing Shala and irresistible food! Is definitely my little place of nirvana in our planet. Looking forward to come back there!"
Erika Hernandez
Quadalajara, Mexico
"The Shala Bali is a true retreat, nestled in the rice fields of Ubud, I could enjoy a real retreat experience,

into the heart of Ubud township. The rooms, shala, kitchen/dining area, salt water pool and landscaping
were all beautiful and comfortable. The staff were lovely, meeting any needs I had, and along with the pets,
created a nurturing environment that felt like home."
